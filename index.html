About Me

I'm a tech enthusiast and content creator from Kochi, Kerala and helping people with my limited knowledge is something I truly love.

I love taking photos and videos of the moments I never want to forget. And when a photo isn't enough, I write it down. That's the root of my short stories and memoirs!
